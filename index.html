Welcome to Pawadise!

About us! We are a community of animal lovers, through and through! We started this company in 1991 with the desire to give back to the animals that have given us so much joy! We are dedicated to providing healthy foods and affordable merchandise for pets of all shapes and sizes!

What our shop offers!

  • Adoptions!
  • Pet food!
  • Pet products. Kennels, collars, toys. etc.
  • We even do pet cleanings and grooming!

Available Pets!

  • Dogs
  • Cats
  • Snakes
  • Lizards

How to adopt!!!

  1. Apply for adoption!
  2. Visit the store!
  3. Pick your buddy!
  4. Check out at the register and recieve your free bag of food with every adoption!
Pet care tips!
  • Visit vet and keep up-to-date on shots!
  • Provide a balanced diet!
  • Ensure proper housing and environment!
  • Regular exercise and playtime!
